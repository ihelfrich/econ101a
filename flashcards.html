Spaced repetition works when you review at intervals — coming back tomorrow is the point. You can switch to Cram mode for unrestricted review, or check Weak cards only to drill what you've gotten wrong recently.

The science

  • Retrieval > rereading. Pulling the answer from memory (even if you fail) builds the trace better than restudying.
  • Spacing > cramming. Each correct recall lengthens the next interval — review at the moment you're about to forget.
  • Rate honestly. Marking "easy" when you struggled poisons the schedule. The system is forgiving; the cards come back.
  • Interleave decks. Mixing consumer / producer / GE forces discrimination and beats blocked practice for transfer.
